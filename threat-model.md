# WHISPRYN Threat Model

**Asset:** Audio recordings and labelled sound samples collected using WHISPRYN's ReSpeaker Mic Array v3.0 and Raspberry Pi 5.

**Threat:** The microphone may accidentally record private conversations or people who have not given consent. Raw recordings could also be exposed if uploaded to our public GitHub repository.

**Response:** Collect audio samples with consent in a controlled environment, avoid recording bystanders, process audio locally where possible, keep raw recordings out of the public repository, and delete recordings when no longer needed.
